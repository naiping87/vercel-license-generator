FAQ

What is the 🚀 Ignition tab? A pre-breakout detector: it ranks stocks by Relative Strength vs the whole market (Leader / Emerging Leader / Weakening), checks sector strength, base quality (volume dry-up, higher low), closing strength (close near day high), and computes a measured-move risk/reward — it tells you why a stock is worth watching, not just that it scored high.

What the number actually means

No look-ahead. Every calculation uses only bars that had already closed, a Yahoo bar with a null close is dropped rather than filled in, and suspensions or gaps are handled with aligned dates and the last two valid prices.

Big volume is not automatically good. Effort is compared with result: heavy volume that closes flat with a long upper wick reads as potential supply, not accumulation - and one high-volume bar never carries a signal on its own.

Leadership with the sector context. Relative strength is measured against the market and against the stock's own sector, with a 20/60-day change in RS rank; a strong sector never adds points to a stock that is lagging it.

Also in the box: live search and sector filter · CSV export of any tab · cancel and retry mid-run · auto-refresh · weekly KDJ golden-cross tray alerts · a daily/weekly chart for every row.

What this is — and what it is not

It narrows 1,000+ stocks down to a shortlist worth charting. It does not predict which stock will explode. Measured on Bursa 2019–2026, the ranking’s average excess return versus the market is about zero, and the list covers roughly 1 in 25 of the stocks that later run. It saves you hours; it does not make the decision for you. Data is for reference only — not investment advice.

The TradingView link is a bonus, not a requirement: it needs a one-time setup, and the app works without it (new tab) and without TradingView at all (built-in chart).
